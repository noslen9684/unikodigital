FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does a website take?

Most sites launch in 2–6 weeks depending on size. You'll get a clear timeline before we start.

Do you work with clients outside the Dominican Republic?

Yes! We work remotely with businesses in the US, Puerto Rico, Europe and across Latin America — in English or Spanish.

Can I update the website myself?

Absolutely. We build on easy-to-edit platforms and give you a short training session at launch.

How do payments work?

Usually 50% to start and 50% at launch. We accept bank transfer (USD or DOP), card and PayPal.

Are hosting and domain included?

We set everything up for you. Hosting and domain fees are billed by the provider, usually $10–30/month.
